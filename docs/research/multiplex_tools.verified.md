## Errors and weak points found

1. Olivar comparison in 4.6 is confounded. `badness_olivar(a,b)` scores the set {a,b}, which includes a-a and b-b self terms, while d3 is cross-only. Cross-only Olivar Badness gives Spearman 0.45 to 0.47 and recall 0.36 (not 0.26 and 0.16), close to the SADDLE formula (0.55, 0.39). Fixed in exp5_scores_fixed.py.
2. "Not good surrogates" is judged against primer3 d3, not against wet-lab data. SADDLE reports AUROC 0.958 for Badness against observed dimer reads (PS1); d3 has no comparable validation in this report. Agreement with primer3 is not accuracy.
3. d3 is 3'-anchored, not extensibility-aware (claim 8). If extensibility matters (PrimerROC), require that the partner has at least 1 nt beyond the base paired to the 3' terminus, for example by checking the structure from `calc_heterodimer(..., output_structure=True)`.
4. The -6 hard cap is not the NGS-PrimerPlex criterion (claim 5); the thresholds table in 5.2 should say "-6 on d3 (stricter than NGS-PrimerPlex's -6 on its short-segment score)".
5. 3'-terminal mismatch structures score 0 in calc_end_stability. With proofreading enzymes (Q5, KAPA HiFi, Vent is exo- but the user's default is Q5-like), 3'-5' exonuclease can trim mismatches and make these extensible, as the SADDLE paper itself notes. Consider also scoring with the last 1 to 2 bases trimmed.
6. PrimerROC also found that ΔG computed at the actual PCR salt performed worse than standard ΔG°37 at 1 M Na. The report recommends the user's real mv/dv; this conflicts with the only empirical dimer-ROC study it cites and should be stated as an open question.
7. Olivar NUMSTEPS wording (claim 3) and "0 or 2 pools" in 5.5 item 5 (should be "1 or 2").
8. The 302 vs 250 bp boundary is sequence-dependent (claim 13).

## Missed points

- primer3 thal 60-nt limit breaks tailed scoring for longer primers; cap gene-specific primer length at 26 nt with Illumina tails, or score tail+primer against the bare partner.
- SADDLE observed the top dimer with only a 5-nt 3' overlap and found its Badness over-weighted 3'-unpaired structures; no score in the report explains the strongest observed dimers either.
- SADDLE and Olivar optimise at low primer concentration (median 45 nM, Olivar uses conc as a multiplier); the report's dna_conc 250 nM default affects primer3 Tm-like outputs but not dG, so per-primer nM matters mainly for fraction-bound terms.
- The exp3 / exp6 "worst d3" is within-pool only; cross-pool pairs are ignored by design, which is right for separate tubes but must be stated in the user-facing report.
- No test of off-target products (OT term) or the 4-mer prefilter was run; both remain unvalidated design proposals, as the report itself says.
